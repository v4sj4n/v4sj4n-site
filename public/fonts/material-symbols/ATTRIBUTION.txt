material-symbols-rounded-subset.woff2
Subset of "Material Symbols Rounded" by Google (material.symbols variable
icon font), used for the site's icon ligatures.

Source: https://fonts.google.com/icons
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org)
Full license text: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/blob/master/OFL.txt

Subset (via fontTools) keeps only the ligatures used on this site plus the
variable axes (opsz, wght, FILL, GRAD):
arrow_outward, terminal, deployed_code, sdk, neurology, dark_mode,
light_mode, check, code_blocks, trending_up, search, close, language,
keyboard_arrow_down, fork_right
